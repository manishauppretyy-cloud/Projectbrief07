Project Title
Job Portal System
1.	Problem Statement
Traditional job searching and recruitment processes are often time-consuming, inefficient, and dependent on manual communication. Job seekers struggle to find suitable opportunities, while employers face challenges in reaching qualified candidates quickly. There is a need for a centralized online platform that streamlines the recruitment process by allowing employers to post job vacancies and job seekers to search and apply for jobs efficiently.
2.	Objective
The main objectives of the Job Portal System are:
•	To provide an online platform for employers to post and manage job vacancies. 
•	To enable job seekers to create profiles, upload resumes, and apply for jobs. 
•	To improve communication between employers and job seekers. 
•	To provide secure and efficient management of job listings and applications. 
3.	Target Users
The primary users of the system are:
•	Job Seekers: Search for jobs, create profiles, upload resumes, and apply for vacancies. 
•	Employers/Recruiters: Register, post job openings, manage applications, and shortlist candidates. 
•	Administrator: Manage users, job postings, companies, report and monitor the overall system. 
4.	Core Modules
1.	Job Seeker Module 
o	Profile creation and management 
o	Resume upload 
o	Job search using filters 
o	Apply for jobs 
o	Track application status 
2.	Employer Module 
o	Company profile management 
o	Post, edit, and delete job vacancies 
o	View and manage applications 
o	Shortlist candidates 
3.	Admin Module 
o	Manage users and employers 
o	Approve or remove job postings 
o	Monitor system activities 
o	Generate reports 
4.	Job Management Module 
o	Add and categorize job listings 
o	Search and filter jobs 
o	Display job details 
5.	Application Management Module 
o	Submit job applications 
o	Track application history 
o	Manage application status 
5.	Scope
The Job Portal System provides a centralized web-based platform for connecting job seekers with employers. It supports user registration, job posting, resume management, job searching, online applications, and application tracking. The system aims to improve recruitment efficiency by reducing manual work and enabling faster communication. Future enhancements may include AI-based job recommendations, interview scheduling, email notifications, and mobile application support.
. Expected Outcome
Upon successful implementation, the Job Portal System will:
•	Provide an efficient online recruitment platform. 
•	Reduce the time required for hiring and job searching. 
•	Enable employers to manage job postings and applications easily. 
•	Allow job seekers to apply for jobs anytime and from anywhere. 
•	Improve transparency and communication throughout the recruitment process. 
•	Offer a secure, user-friendly, and scalable system for employment management.
